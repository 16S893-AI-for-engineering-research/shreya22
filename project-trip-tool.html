Trip tool

Pick two of the 50 busiest US airports and compare one passenger's trip under the two network structures: point-to-point (a nonstop) and hub-and-spoke (one connection). Fuel and NOx come from OpenAP for the aircraft that actually fly each leg in the T-100 data.

Read this first

  • These are intermediate model outputs, not findings. The project's comparison metric is population-weighted exposure (ADR 0003), which has not been computed yet. Fuel and NOx per passenger are only the inputs to it.
  • One quarter only: 2019 Q1 (T-100 segments, DB1B 10% sample scaled ×10).
  • Idealized flights: OpenAP synthetic flights at 80% MTOW, no wind.
  • Aircraft with no OpenAP model (turboprops, business jets, some older types) are excluded from fuel, not estimated, and are not rescaled to the other types. The share is shown on every leg.
  • Per passenger = leg fuel (or NOx) per flight ÷ (seats × load factor), where seats and load factor are T-100 averages for that segment.
  • Point-to-point is only a nonstop when one was actually observed (at least 13 departures in the quarter); otherwise it is the same connecting trip (ADR 0007).
