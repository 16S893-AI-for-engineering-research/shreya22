Air Quality Impacts of Changing Airline Network Structures

Project proposal — 16.S893. Scroll top to bottom; this page is the three-minute version of the argument.

01 · The starting point

Air travel demand is not spread evenly.

Take the US as the sample system. A handful of airports carry an enormous share of passengers; most carry very little. Any network you design has to absorb this shape of demand.

02 · Two ways to serve that demand

The same demand, two different network structures.

You can funnel everything through a few consolidated hubs, or you can fly passengers directly between their origin and destination. These are not just business models — they produce different flight counts, different aircraft sizes, and different amounts of fuel burned per passenger-kilometre.

03 · The question

For a fixed demand, how does the choice between hub-and-spoke and point-to-point networks affect air quality?

04 · Why this isn't one knob

Fuel is a good proxy for climate. It is a poor proxy for air quality.

05 · How

Where an agent fits into the work.

A few representative examples of what I plan to hand to an agent over the semester — not an exhaustive list, and likely to grow as the project runs into things I did not anticipate.

01

Scope the question from the ops side

Use an agent to synthesize the airline-operations literature on hub-and-spoke vs. point-to-point network design — new territory for me, since I have only approached this from the regulatory side before — and narrow it into one answerable research question.

02

Turn it into a week-by-week workplan

Have the agent convert the scoped question into a semester-sized plan — weekly milestones, checkpoints, and a stretch goal — so scope stays matched to the time available.

03

Get up to speed on the tools, from source

Read the codebases of the open-source performance and emissions tools I will depend on, using an agent to surface their limitations and undocumented assumptions before I build anything on top of them.

04

Visualize, then build the walkthrough

Generate exploratory visualizations throughout, and eventually an interactive walkthrough of the analysis for the project website. The final write-up stays in my own voice — the agent does not draft the prose.
